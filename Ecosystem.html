About Brain Fit

One credentialed network. Five clinical specialties. One accountable partner.

Brain Fit, LLC is a multi-specialty provider network and behavioral health consulting firm headquartered in the Phoenix metropolitan area. We credential, coordinate, and manage a multi-disciplinary network of licensed providers to fulfill veteran, public-sector, and federal healthcare evaluation contracts under a unified, accountable delivery structure.

Our network spans psychology, dentistry, audiology, optometry, and medical disciplines, with 21 credentialed providers actively deployed across 10 states. Several network psychologists hold PSYPACT authorization, extending licensed telehealth coverage to 40+ states and the District of Columbia.

Core capabilities

What Brain Fit delivers

Provider network management
Nationwide sourcing, credentialing, scheduling, compliance tracking, and quality assurance across a multi-disciplinary licensed provider network.
C&P evaluation delivery
Coordinated Compensation & Pension evaluations for veteran populations through VA and DoD-aligned programs, with full administrative and compliance support.
Clinical assessment & testing
Cognitive, personality, diagnostic, and risk assessment using validated instruments: WAIS-5, WMS-IV, WISC-V, ADOS-2, CARS-2, Conners-4, and more.
Behavioral health consulting
Design, implementation, and program evaluation of trauma-informed, recovery-oriented behavioral health programs for public-sector and healthcare partners.
Training & workforce development
Competency-based clinical training, supervision, and continuing education for behavioral health professionals across multi-site programs.
Project management & administration
Turn-key contract coordination, compliance tracking, quality assurance, scheduling, and data reporting across integrated systems.
